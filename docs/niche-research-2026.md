# Ocean Interactive — Niche & Revenue Research (2025–2026)

> Compiled by `market-scout` from a 5-angle deep-research sweep (hyper-casual publisher economics, work-for-hire, prototype-funding, mobile demand, PC/Steam demand). Every claim is sourced. Estimates are flagged. This is the evidence base for which prototypes the studio is allowed to build under the 90% rule.

## TL;DR — the strategy the data dictates

1. **Revenue assurance comes from the CONTRACT, not the genre.** The only paths that realistically reach 90%+ assurance are **(a) work-for-hire / co-dev with a signed brief + escrow/deposit**, and **(b) publisher "burn-rate / pay-per-prototype" retainers** (e.g., Azur covers a studio's monthly costs after 2–4 prototypes). Everything else — spec mobile hits, spec Steam IP, publisher funding pitches — is a single-digit-percent lottery.
2. **The product specialization that wins us those contracts is HYBRID-CASUAL PUZZLE.** It has the strongest 2025 demand (+100–114% YoY), the lowest build cost, the most predictable monetization, and the most publishers *actively paying to test prototypes* in it.
3. **So Ocean's model:** sell hybrid-casual-puzzle prototyping *as a service* (WFH + publisher retainer) — assured cash — and keep a small, profit-funded moonshot lane for PC co-op/crewlike IP, the highest-upside bet on the board.

## 2. The winning product niche: HYBRID-CASUAL PUZZLE

**Why this specific niche (all 2025 actuals):**
- Mobile revenue is flat (+1% to $82B) and downloads are *shrinking* (−7.2%) — the market pays for **revenue-per-user, not installs** ([Sensor Tower](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-94-billion-in-revenue-in-2025-downloads-reached-52-billion-302696284.html)). Hybrid-casual is the migration target.
- Hybrid-casual IAP grew **+67% (Q1) → +100% (Q2) → +114% (Q3) YoY**; >$345M IAP in H1 2025; ~30% of US App Store revenue *growth* ([AppMagic via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-10-hybrid-casual-games), [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/mobile-game-development-trends-usa-2025/)).
- **Puzzle is 60%+ of all hybrid-casual revenue** (up from 50%) ([AppMagic Q3 via Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/rollic-top-10-hybrid-casual-games/)). Winning cores: **block/sort, screw/bolt-sort, merge-2**. Color Block Jam (Rollic) $33M, Screwdom (iKame) $29M, All in Hole (Homa) $16.5M in a single quarter ([GamerBraves](https://www.gamerbraves.com/appmagic-reveals-top-10-hybridcasual-games-in-q3-2025-and-the-innovations-behind-them/)).
- **Most predictable monetization in mobile:** "fail offers" drive **19–25%+ of IAP** in Rollic's flagships ([AppMagic via GamerBraves](https://www.gamerbraves.com/appmagic-reveals-top-10-hybridcasual-games-in-q3-2025-and-the-innovations-behind-them/)); puzzle runs ~50/50 ads/IAP.
- **Lowest content burden** of any high-demand niche — buildable by a tiny team, template-friendly.
- **Most publishers actively paying to test prototypes here:** Puzzle → Rollic, Voodoo; Idle/arcade → SayGames, Homa, Supercent ([NextBigGames Dec 2025](https://nextbiggames.com/2026/01/19/hybrid-casual-publisher-analysis-december-2025/)). Rollic ideates ~1,000 games/month ([PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-rollic-scored-a-100m-hybridcasual-hit-by-ideating-1000-games-a-month/)).

**The viability bar a prototype must clear (build to these):**
- Retention: **D1 ≥ 35–45%, D7 ≈ 20%, D30 ≥ 10%** ([Game Growth Advisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-04-16-hybrid-casual-game-design-strategy-2026/)).
- Test CPI: **$2.50–$6 Tier-1 / sub-$2.50 Tier-2/3**; hybrid-casual Android CPI ~$0.95 ([Segwise/Liftoff](https://segwise.ai/blog/cpi-ipm-roas-benchmarks-optimizing-ad-spend), [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/casual-games)).
- Rule: **LTV > CPI**, D1 > 30% & D30 > 4% minimum.

**AVOID (saturated/walled):** match-3 (4 of 367 new titles cleared $100k/mo — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/what-leading-match-3-and-merge-games-do-differently/)), 4X strategy (incumbent walled garden — [Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2025/6/5/the-state-of-mobile-gaming-2025)), pure ad-only hypercasual (ATT-killed).

## 3. The moonshot niche (PC, profit-funded only): CO-OP / "CREWLIKE"

If/when runway allows a bet, the data points to one place:
- **Co-op was the #1 PC genre by H1 2025 revenue: $4.1B in six months, +11% YoY** ([ScreenRant/Alinea](https://screenrant.com/co-op-games-steam-2025-4-billion-6-months/)). R.E.P.O. sold 16.4M copies in 6 months.
- Crewlike titles post the **highest wishlist→sales multipliers ever measured: PEAK 266×, R.E.P.O. 68×, Webfishing 67×**, mostly priced sub-$10 ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/which-genres-have-ruled-steam-a-new)).
- **Escape-room co-op is GameDiscoverCo's single best-converting Steam tag** — low developer interest = thin supply ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/which-pc-genres-convert-pre-launch)). The moat is netcode difficulty, which a competent team can cross.
- Adjacent gaps: survival-crafting (median ~$10k, thin supply), automation/factory (strong medians), colony/4X management.
- Match each slice to its publisher: **Hooded Horse** (strategy/colony), **Devolver/Wired** (horror), **11 Bit/Rokaplay** (cozy-hybrid). Publishers in 2025 want **a clean vertical slice + a live wishlist trajectory** ([GameDeveloper](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/meet-the-indie-studios-funding-other-indie-studios)).

PC IP stays a **lottery ticket** (power-law: 8.33% of top-100 indies take 80% of revenue — [VGI](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/assets/reports/VGI_Global_Indie_Games_Market_Report_2024.pdf)). We only buy that ticket with house money.

## 4. Pricing anchors (for `finance-analyst`)

- **WFH studio rates:** Western $60–$200/hr; UK contractor avg ~£438/day ([YunoJuno](https://www.yunojuno.com/freelancer-rates-report)). Eastern Europe $50–$100/hr; Asia $40–$80/hr ([Whimsy Games](https://whimsygames.co/blog/complete-guide-to-game-development-outsourcing-costs/)).
- **Prototype project fees:** minimal <$2k; fuller $10k–$50k ([SDLC Corp](https://sdlccorp.com/post/indie-game-development-cost/)).
- **Publisher prototype micro-payments:** $300/test, up to $5k/iteration (Supersonic); $5k–$10k/month iteration for hybrid hitting KPIs ([Supersonic terms](https://supersonic.com/superhybrid-terms/)).
- **Hybrid-casual rev-share:** CrazyLabs 55% to dev; Homa Jam 50/50 ([PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/55-rev-share-in-the-new-hypercasual-developer-challenge-by-crazylabs/)) — small/no advance outside contests *(industry consensus, not a single contract)*.
- **PC publisher advances** (moonshot reference): Outersloth $50k–$2M, 50%/15% split ([GameDeveloper](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/meet-the-indie-studios-funding-other-indie-studios)); Kowloon Nights $500k–$10M, no-recoup.
- **Advergaming/branded games** (a real WFH pipeline): advergaming $3.71B (2024) → $12.0B (2035) ([Market Research Future](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/in-game-advertising-market-11711)).

## 5. Confidence & caveats
- **Solid 2025–2026 actuals:** all hybrid-casual growth %, puzzle revenue share, retention/CPI bars, Steam median-revenue/failure stats, co-op $4.1B, Azur/Supersonic/CrazyLabs/Homa published terms, Kepler 0.2% signing rate.
- **Estimates / soft:** total outsourcing & match-3 market sizes (analyst ranges diverge widely — trust store-tracked AppMagic/Gamalytic over projections); "standard" (non-contest) rev-shares for Voodoo/SayGames/Rollic/Kwalee are private and inferred; per-genre Steam median dollar figures are aggregator point estimates.
- **Action before any real funding decision:** spot-verify live dashboards (gamalytic.com/genres, appmagic.rocks, games-stats.com) — several primary sources block automated fetch.
