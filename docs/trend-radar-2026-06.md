# Ocean Interactive — Trend Radar (June 2026)

> What's surging *right now* on mobile + PC, scored against the **>90% build rule**. Sourced; estimates flagged. Compiled by `market-scout` from two live research sweeps.

## The core finding (read this first)

"Trending now" tells us **which** niche, not **whether we'll get paid**. A flock downloading the category *leader* does not transfer to our new entry. So a surging trend, on its own, scores a candidate at roughly **30–60% confidence — it fails the >90% rule.** The trend only crosses 90% when it is **paired with a committed payer**: a signed work-for-hire/co-dev brief, or an accepted slot in a publisher program that *pays to test/fund prototypes in that exact niche.*

The good news from the research: **in the two hottest niches, those paying buyers exist and are actively hungry right now.** That overlap is the whole game.

## Confidence scoring against the >90% rule (honest)

| Path | Confidence of revenue | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Build a hot-niche game **on spec**, hope it hits / hope a publisher bites | **15–45%** (even in a surging niche — survivorship bias; for every R.E.P.O. there are hundreds in Steam's bottom half) | ❌ **NO-BUILD** under the rule |
| Build mobile puzzle forks **under a burn-rate / pay-per-test relationship** (Azur/Supersonic) | **~90%+** (payment is structural/contractual; you're paid to produce, hit-scaling is upside) | ✅ buildable once the relationship is signed |
| Build a PC slice **under a signed publisher advance / co-dev brief** in a funded niche | **~92–97%** (contractual) | ✅ buildable once the brief is signed |

**The disciplined read:** R.E.P.O./PEAK being spec successes is exactly the trap the rule guards against — they're the lottery winners, not the expected outcome. We capture the *same demand* without the *same risk* by securing the payer first.

## Where surging demand and a paying buyer actually overlap (the two real shots)

- **ARENA A — Mobile:** sort/screw/block **puzzle forks** → Supersonic / Homa / Kwalee / **Azur burn-rate**. Cheap (2–4 wk), fast feedback, structured per-prototype pay. A *volume* engine: many cheap shots into proven demand waves, with payment attached per prototype.
- **ARENA B — PC:** **co-op social/physics horror** slice → Kinetic / Pocketpair / Outersloth. Higher ceiling, ~4–10 wk slice, publishers are *flooded with appetite* for this exact thing and fund small teams.

## Rule-compliant next action (does NOT start a build)
1. `market-scout` + `deal-closer`: pick ONE arena, take the **concept + a 60–90s proof hook** to the named buyers, and secure a *commitment* (program slot / burn-rate terms / co-dev brief + advance).
2. `finance-analyst`: only when that commitment is in writing does the confidence cross 90%.
3. `playtest-qa`: pre-build QA review.
4. *Then* — and only then — `prototype-engineer` builds.

This is the version of your "ride the trend" thesis that survives the >90% rule: **ride the trend into a buyer's open hand, not into the void.**
