# Ocean Interactive — Business Plan (v1)

## 1. Thesis
Finished games are slow, expensive, and risky. **Proven prototypes** are fast, cheap, and de-risk the buyer. There is a real, repeatable market of buyers who pay for proven concepts: publishers hunting for their next title, investors funding teams with traction, and studios that outsource prototyping. We sell shovels to the gold rush.

## 2. What we sell
A **prototype**: a playable vertical slice that proves one magic moment, plus the proof pack (playtest data + hero shot + market fit). We sell the *concept and its proof*, not a shippable product.

## 3. Customers ("the right customers")
| Segment | Pays for | Why they buy |
|---|---|---|
| Hyper-casual / casual mobile publishers | CPI-testable prototypes | They test dozens of concepts monthly; cheap proven hooks are their lifeblood |
| Premium PC/console indie publishers | Vertical slices with a fresh hook | They fund promising slices into full games |
| Investors / accelerators | A demo proving a team can execute | They fund teams, and a slice is the cheapest proof of competence |
| Other studios / brands (work-for-hire) | Prototypes built to their brief | They lack bandwidth to prototype internally |

## 4. Revenue model (three paths)
1. **Outright sale / IP assignment** — buyer pays a flat fee for the prototype + IP. Clean cash, no upside. Use for runway.
2. **License / co-development** — modest advance + royalty (typ. single-digit to low-double-digit %). Use for high-upside IP we believe in.
3. **Work-for-hire** — client pays a day rate / fixed scope to build prototypes to their brief. Steady cash that funds paths 1 & 2.

Default portfolio mix early on: **~60% work-for-hire** (keeps lights on) / **~30% outright sales** / **~10% licensing bets** (lottery tickets with upside). Rebalance toward licensing as runway grows.

## 5. Why we win
- **Throughput, not perfection.** We optimize prototypes shipped per quarter, each with a credible buyer.
- **Discipline gates.** Finance + market validation kill losers before they cost money.
- **Sell the feeling.** Browser-playable builds + hero GIFs convert faster than pitch text.
- **Clean IP.** Royalty-free/ownable assets so every prototype can be sold without legal drag.

## 6. Operating rhythm
- Run **2–4 prototypes in parallel**, each at a different pipeline stage.
- Weekly portfolio review (`studio-head`): advance, hold, or kill each prototype by its gate.
- Reinvest revenue into the next batch of prototypes.

## 7. Risks & mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| No buyer materializes | `market-scout` validates a named buyer *before* build (Concept gate) |
| Build cost overruns | Hard scope guardrails in GDD; disposable code; slice-only builds |
| Race to the bottom on price | `finance-analyst` walk-away prices; lean on licensing upside |
| Feast/famine cash flow | Work-for-hire base load smooths revenue |
| IP/legal drag on sale | Ownable assets + clean licenses enforced by art-director/engineer |

## 8. 90-day plan
- **Days 1–30:** Land 1 work-for-hire client (cash). Ship 2 spec prototypes to portfolio.
- **Days 31–60:** Submit spec prototypes to 5+ named publishers/portals. Close first outright sale.
- **Days 61–90:** Sign 1 licensing/co-dev term sheet. Reach cash-flow positive on a monthly basis.

Success metric: **monthly revenue > monthly burn**, and ≥1 prototype sold or licensed.
