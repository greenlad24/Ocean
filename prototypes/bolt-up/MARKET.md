*owner: market-scout · evidence base: docs/niche-research-2026.md*

## Buyer segments (ranked)
1. **Hybrid-casual puzzle publishers running prototype-test / burn-rate programs** — the primary, near-assured buyers. They pay to test and iterate, and the genre is their top revenue driver.
2. **Publishers seeking a fresh sort-mechanic twist** — "sort" is hot but commoditised; the rust-cascade is a differentiator they can creative-test.
3. **White-label / work-for-hire clients** who want a proven sort-puzzle base reskinned to a brief (advergaming, IP tie-ins).

## Comparable deals / market signal (2025 actuals)
- Hybrid-casual puzzle = **60%+ of all hybrid-casual revenue**; genre IAP +100–114% YoY through 2025.
- Direct comps: Color Block Jam $33M, Screwdom $29M, All in Hole $16.5M — *single quarter* (AppMagic Q3 2025).
- "Fail offers" (our stubbed mechanic) drive **19–25% of IAP** in flagship hybrid puzzlers.
- Per-prototype payment is repeatable (Supersonic $300/test → $5k iterate; Azur burn-rate); the *scaled hit* is ~1% lottery. We sell the assured layer, not the lottery.

## Viability bar (what a buyer will test against)
D1 ≥ 35–45%, D7 ≈ 20%, D30 ≥ 10%; test CPI sub-$2.50 (Tier 2/3). Sort puzzles are highly ad-creative-friendly → strong IPM, which lowers CPI.

## Price anchor → handoff to finance-analyst
- **Per-test / iteration micro-payments:** $300–$5k (Supersonic-class), $5–10k/mo for hybrid iteration hitting KPIs.
- **Burn-rate retainer:** monthly studio-cost coverage after 2–4 prototypes (Azur-class) — the assured income.
- **Rev-share upside (moonshot):** 50–55% of net if it scales (CrazyLabs/Homa) — not counted toward the RAS floor.

## Honest read
Bolt Up is a **calling-card prototype**: its job is to (a) prove the rust-cascade hook and (b) open a *burn-rate / PPP relationship* that converts future prototypes into ~90%+ assured income. A single submission's odds of a scaled hit are ~1% — so we do **not** value this on a speculative sale; we value it as the asset that lands the assured contract.
