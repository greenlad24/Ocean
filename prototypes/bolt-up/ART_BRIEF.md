## Shape language & readability
Bolts are fat hex-head discs stacked on vertical rods — colour = identity, hex silhouette = "hardware" theme. A completed rod gets a subtle gold ring so "solved" is unmistakable. Rusted bolts are desaturated + cross-hatch so the player instantly reads "locked, not mine yet."

## UX flow (minimum screens)
`Title (tap to play)` → `Level (the whole game)` → `Win burst → next level` / `Stuck → fail-offer overlay`. The magic moment lives mid-level on every lock.

## Asset list
- **Needed for the slice (all primitives/code-drawn):** bolts (hex + highlight), rods, pegboard bg, gold lock-ring, rust hatch, spark particles, lift/drop tween.
- **Faked / deferred:** real textures, sound design, character/mascot, store icon set.

## Polish-for-pitch (the 3 cheapest value multipliers)
1. **Lock pop** — scale-punch + screwshake + particle burst on rod completion.
2. **De-rust sparkle** — bright spark + colour-bloom when a rusted bolt frees (sells the cascade).
3. **Snappy tweens + a chunky font** — perceived quality jumps with easing curves and one good typeface.

## Marketing shot (hero frame for the deck)
A mid-cascade freeze: one rod locking gold while two neighbouring rusted bolts spark free — captioned *"one solve, three unlocks."* This is the deck thumbnail, the store screenshot, and the ad creative.
