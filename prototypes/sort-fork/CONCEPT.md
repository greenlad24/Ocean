## The strategy (read before the concept)
We are **not** betting on guessing the one viral skin. The mobile-puzzle research is explicit: the sort/clear core is proven (+222% YoY), and a *novel skin* opens a 6–8 week first-mover CPI window (the "Wool Sort" pattern). The durable, >90% play is **a pay-per-prototype / burn-rate relationship** with a publisher, under which we ship a *stream* of cheap forks into proven demand and get paid per prototype — hits are upside, not the basis for revenue. **The concept below is the lead pitch artifact for securing that relationship, not a single all-in bet.**

## The proven core (what we don't reinvent)
Move items between containers; an item only lands on a matching item (or empty); fill a container with one type → it **locks** with a satisfying pop. Billions of installs (water/ball/screw/block sort). Cheap, instantly readable, ad-creative gold.

## The fork hypothesis — lead + alternates
The lever that moves CPI is the **skin**: it must (a) be visceral/relatable on a 15-second video, (b) make the "before→after" satisfying, (c) be visually unoccupied right now. Three candidates, ranked by my honest read of ad-creative power vs. build cost:

1. **LEAD — "Cable Sort" / Untangle:** sort tangled, colour-coded charging cables/wires into matching ports; the untangle animation resolves the universal mild-anxiety of a cable knot. High relatability, strong before→after, low build cost.
2. **"Polish Sort" (nail/makeup):** sort colour bottles onto shelves; taps the large casual-female audience + ASMR satisfaction; excellent aspirational ad creative.
3. **"Keyring Sort":** sort keys by shape/colour onto rings with a satisfying jingle; cheap, novel, tactile.

## Magic moment (the one thing to prove)
The **lock-pop + the skin's signature resolution** (the cable visibly de-tangling / the shelf snapping tidy). If the skin's resolution isn't *more* satisfying than a plain colour-sort, the fork has no CPI edge and we drop it.

## Viability bar (what a CPI test will judge)
Target **Android CPI < $0.40** (sub-$0.30 to greenlight at Supersonic), **D1 ≥ 35%**, **IPM high** (the skin's job). LTV > CPI before any scale.

## Honest risks
- **Picking the winning skin is a creative gamble** (~low per-skin hit rate). Mitigation: the volume-under-a-paid-relationship model, not one bet.
- The skin may already exist or be in a competitor's pipeline — `market-scout` must confirm whitespace per skin before it's pitched.
- Sort puzzles are commoditised; without a genuinely fresher resolution, this is just another clone.

## Self-QA (game-designer + market-scout)
- *Assumptions:* sort-core demand persists through 2026 (evidenced, +222% YoY); a novel skin still opens a CPI window (evidenced, Wool Sort, but windows are shortening as incumbents spend $9M+/mo on UA in hot forks).
- *Confidence this concept, built on spec, becomes a profitable game:* **~25–40%** — fails the >90% rule on its own. *Confidence it's a strong enough artifact to help land a paid relationship:* **~60%.**
- *What would make this wrong:* the chosen skin already exists / doesn't beat plain sort on satisfaction / the CPI window has closed. Each is cheaply testable (competitor scan + a fake-door creative test) **before** any full build.
- *Not done until:* `market-scout` confirms skin whitespace and `finance-analyst` signs the >90% gate. Until then: **do not build.**
